# TrueNorth Frames — Photographer Recruitment Outreach

**Goal:** Recruit Edmonton (YEG) photographers as founding sellers during Phase 1 (free land-grab).
**Core value prop to lead with:**
- **Local-first** — built only for Edmonton, not a generic national tool like Snappr.
- **Free to join, no commission (Phase 1)** — no fees, no lock-in. Founding members get grandfathered perks.
- **Trust Aggregator** — your Google, Instagram, Yelp & Facebook reputation combined into one composite Trust Score on your profile. Your reviews already exist — we put them to work in one place.
- Free portfolio, packages, booking calendar, and client messaging tools.

---

## 1. Cold Email

### Subject line options
- `Building an Edmonton-only photographer marketplace — want a founding spot?`
- `{FirstName}, your Google + IG reviews in one place (Edmonton photographers)`
- `A local alternative to Snappr — free founding invite for {BusinessName}`
- `Quick idea for {BusinessName} — Edmonton photographer marketplace`

### Body (primary version)

> **Subject: Building an Edmonton-only photographer marketplace — want a founding spot?**
>
> Hi {FirstName},
>
> I came across {BusinessName} while looking at Edmonton's best {specialty} photographers — your {specific detail, e.g. "outdoor family work" / "editorial maternity portraits"} stood out.
>
> I'm building **TrueNorth Frames**, a marketplace made *only* for Edmonton photographers and clients — not a national platform where you're competing with everyone from Toronto to Vancouver. The idea is to own the local market before someone else does.
>
> Two things I think you'll care about:
>
> 1. **It's free to join right now, with no booking commission.** We're in a founding-member phase — early photographers get grandfathered perks when paid tiers launch.
> 2. **We pull your reputation together.** Your reviews are scattered across Google, Instagram, Yelp and Facebook. TrueNorth combines them into a single **Trust Score** on your profile, so clients see your full track record in one glance.
>
> You'd get a free portfolio, bookable packages, an availability calendar, and direct client messaging.
>
> Would you be open to a founding profile? I can set one up in ~10 minutes, or send you the link to claim it yourself. Happy to answer anything.
>
> Cheers,
> {YourName}
> TrueNorth Frames — https://truenorthframes.vercel.app
> {phone / email}

## 2. Instagram DM

Keep it short, human, mobile-first. No links in the first DM (IG suppresses/penalizes them) — offer the link once they reply.

### Opener A (warm, compliment-led)

> Hey {FirstName}! 👋 Love your {specialty} work — the {specific shot/series} caught my eye. I'm building **TrueNorth Frames**, a photographer marketplace made *only* for Edmonton. We're inviting a small founding group — free to join, no commission. Mind if I send a couple details?

### Opener B (value-led, for busy pros)

> Hi {FirstName}! Quick one — I'm launching an Edmonton-only photographer marketplace (think a local alternative to Snappr, minus the fees). It pulls your Google + IG + Yelp reviews into one Trust Score on your profile. Free founding spot for {BusinessName} if you're interested — want the rundown?

### Reply once they respond

> Amazing 🙌 Here's the gist: free profile, bookable packages, availability calendar, and client messaging — all Edmonton-focused. Founding members get grandfathered perks when paid tiers launch later. Here's the site: truenorthframes.vercel.app — want me to set up your profile or send you a claim link?

## 3. Personalization tokens (fill per prospect)

| Token | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `{FirstName}` | Photographer's first name |
| `{BusinessName}` | Studio / brand name |
| `{specialty}` | wedding / family / newborn / maternity / boudoir / branding / real estate |
| `{specific detail}` | A genuine detail from their portfolio (proves it's not mass mail) |

**Rule of thumb:** every message needs *one* true, specific observation about their work. It's the single biggest driver of reply rates for cold photographer outreach.
